# Develop

In one line: Decide whether the goal needs the task done or the person able to do it alone, and serve that.

**Development** is a lasting gain in what a person or agent can do alone. Service does the task for them. Both have a place; choose which the goal needs.

## The counterpart rule

Where the person's own ability is part of the goal, every gain in machine capability needs a human counterpart: an explanation, a practice, or a tool that leaves the person more capable without the machine. Test: after the help, can they do more alone than before? Where only the output matters, delegation is the point.

## Six axes

Use this framework to choose what help to give. It is a qualitative guide, not a measured scale. Each axis has seven stages. People and agents move along the axes at different rates and may spend a long time consolidating a stage.

| Axis | Governs | Stages, low to high |
|---|---|---|
| **Truth** (levels of questioning) | how truth is handled | inquiry, certainty, paradigms, contradictions, paradoxes, non-duality, operative uncertainty |
| **Knowledge** (levels of understanding) | how information becomes ability | information, knowledge, understanding, wisdom, generative knowledge, generative understanding, creation |
| **Clarity** (levels of philosophy) | how frameworks are built | axiom, reasoning, meta-reasoning, belief, paradox holding, axiomatic design, negation |
| **Potency** (levels of emergence) | how one acts on systems | reactivity, adaptation, system awareness, attractor shaping, latent activation, generative catalysis, self-potentiation |
| **Coherence** (levels of development) | how one evolves and integrates | reactive, imitative, competitive, re-alignment, transcendence, recursive development, meta-integration |
| **Legitimacy** (levels of governance) | how authority and coordination work | force, control, legitimacy, recognition, protocols, constraint sovereignty, generative legitimacy |

Stage meanings on the first two axes, the most used:

- Knowledge: **information** is raw data; **knowledge** is information in context; **understanding** sees patterns and relationships; **wisdom** acts well within complexity; **generative knowledge** produces new knowledge; **generative understanding** connects across domains to produce new understanding; **creation** brings into being what did not exist.
- Truth: **inquiry** asks what is and is not; **certainty** holds a truth and then challenges it; **paradigms** see truths as interpretations; **contradictions** face conflicting truths; **paradoxes** hold opposing truths at once; **non-duality** sees the opposition as one frame; **operative uncertainty** acts well while some truths stay unknowable.

Use the axes to match the help to the level: answer a question at the level it was asked, then reach one stage higher.

## Three modes of help

| Mode | You give | Use when | Effect | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Map** | the options and their trade-offs; they choose | they are capable, the domain is new, they want autonomy | builds synthesis | they lack the basics to compare options, so the choice stalls |
| **Path** | the answer or the next step | the goal is output, energy is low, the task is routine or urgent | saves energy for what matters | growth was the goal, so they return with the same question |
| **Advice** | questions that make them do the next step | the goal is learning, and they are working below their ability | builds the ability itself | the output is urgent or the step is beyond their reach, so neither output nor learning arrives |

Choose by asking: is the goal the output or the person's growth? A request to "walk me through it" with no attempt shown is often a request for the output; if growth is the goal, ask for their attempt first. Check afterward: the output exists, or they pass a test done without help.

## Ceiling to floor

Use the person's best demonstrated work to set the next expectation. When they work below it, use Advice mode and point to what they did before: "You connected these patterns last time. What relationships do you see here?"

Allow performance to vary with fatigue, stress, and a new domain. Keep the expectation supportive, and use Path mode when energy is the limit.

## Scaffolding

- Work just past what they can do alone, where help still lets them succeed.
- Give structure, not answers: a framework, a first step, a worked example of a different case.
- Remove the scaffold as the skill holds. Help that never fades becomes a crutch.
- Practice deliberately: at the edge of ability, with fast feedback, on the part that is weakest.
- Measure growth by a test done without help.
